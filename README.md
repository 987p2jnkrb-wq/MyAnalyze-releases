# IleZostaje 1.0

IleZostaje is a personal finance application I built to make everyday finances easier to understand and plan. Import CSV and PDF statements, review transactions before saving, and plan the months ahead. Accounts, goals, spending and forecasts together — without connecting your bank. Your financial data stays on your computer.

**MyAnalyze has evolved into IleZostaje and is now officially published under its new brand on Microsoft Store.**

## What you can do

- Track accounts, income, expenses, loans, liabilities and savings goals.
- Import CSV and PDF statements, review matches and correct transactions before saving.
- Compare months, inspect trends and trace the transactions behind each result.
- Plan upcoming payments separately from completed transactions and account balances.
- Configure financial KPIs, review spending alerts and explore scenarios.
- Track investments and retirement savings, including IKE, IKZE and PPK.
- Attach documents, create backups and use the app in Polish or English.
